Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

This policy explains what personal information SAMRIDGE collects when you use this website, why we collect it, and what your rights are. We aim to comply with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

1. Who we are

SAMRIDGE is a website design and digital services business based in Nigeria. We are responsible for the personal information you give us through this website. You can contact us at contact.samridge@gmail.com or on WhatsApp at +234 916 793 0217.

2. What information we collect

  • Enquiry form: your name, email address, the service you need, your message, and your consent.
  • Direct messages: if you email us or message us on WhatsApp, we receive the details you send, such as your name, phone number and message.
  • Technical data: like most websites, the company that hosts this site may automatically record technical details such as your IP address and browser type in server logs. We do not control or use this data for profiling.

We only ask for what we need to reply to you. Please do not send passwords, payment card details or other sensitive information through the form.

3. Why we use your information

  • To reply to your enquiry and prepare a plan or quote (based on your consent and on steps you ask us to take before entering a contract).
  • To deliver services if you become a client (to perform our contract with you).
  • To keep business, tax and accounting records (to meet legal obligations).
  • To protect the website from abuse and spam (our legitimate interest).

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Who we share it with

  • Formspree, the service that delivers form submissions to us. It processes your submission under its own privacy terms.
  • GitHub Pages, which hosts this website.
  • Gmail and WhatsApp, if you contact us by email or WhatsApp. These services have their own privacy policies.
  • Image providers (Unsplash and Pexels): some pictures on this website are loaded from their servers, so your IP address and browser details may be received by them when those images load.

We may also share information where the law requires it.

5. Transfers outside Nigeria

Some of the services above process data outside Nigeria, including in the United States. We use them because they are widely used, and we only send them what is needed to provide the service.

6. How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that do not lead to a project: up to 12 months, then deleted.
  • Client records: for as long as we work together, and afterwards for as long as needed for legal, tax and accounting purposes.

7. Cookies and tracking

This website does not set cookies of its own and does not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools. If this changes, we will update this policy and ask for your consent where required.

8. Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, you may have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we hold about you and get a copy
  • Ask us to correct information that is wrong
  • Ask us to delete your information, or to restrict how we use it
  • Object to certain uses of your information
  • Withdraw your consent at any time

To use any of these rights, email us at contact.samridge@gmail.com. If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, you can also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

9. Security

We take sensible steps to protect your information, including using trusted services and limiting who can see enquiries. No method of sending data over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Children

This website is intended for adults and businesses. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.

12. Contact us

Questions about this policy? Email contact.samridge@gmail.com or message us on WhatsApp.
