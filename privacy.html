Privacy Notice

Last updated: 23 November 2026

SAMRIDGE respects your privacy. This notice explains what information may be collected when you use our website and contact us.

1. Information We Collect

When you submit a website enquiry, we may collect your name, email address, project type and the message you provide.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use enquiry information to read, respond to and manage requests about our digital services. We do not ask you to provide unnecessary personal information.

3. Form Processing

Our contact form is powered by Formspree. Information submitted through the form is processed by Formspree and may be stored or transmitted through its systems before being delivered to us. Formspree's own privacy practices also apply.

You can read more in Formspree's Privacy Policy .

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your enquiry information. It may be handled by service providers involved in operating the contact form and delivering messages.

5. Data Retention and Security

Enquiry information should only be kept for as long as needed to respond to requests and manage related communications. We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

6. Your Choices and Requests

You may contact us to ask about personal information you have submitted or request that it be corrected or deleted, subject to applicable legal requirements and information held by our service providers.

7. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact: contact.samridge@gmail.com .
