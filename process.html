HOW WE WORK

From idea to live website in four clear steps.

No confusing jargon and no surprises. Here is exactly what happens after you get in touch.

01

Understand

It starts with your enquiry. We read what you sent, and if we need more detail we ask a few simple questions about your goals, your audience and what you want your website to achieve.

  • What is your business, idea or work about?
  • Who do you want to reach?
  • What should visitors do: call, order, book or enquire?
  • Do you have a logo, colours or examples you like?

What we need from you: a short description of your idea and any examples you like.

02

Plan

We organise your ideas into a clear plan: which pages you need, what each page should say, and the look and feel that suits your brand. Then we send you a quote and timeline.

  • The pages and sections your website will have
  • The design direction, based on the concepts you liked
  • A clear price and expected timeline

What we need from you: your approval of the plan and quote before we begin building.

03

Create

Once the plan is agreed, we build your website. We design it to work well on phones first, then check that it looks great on tablets and computers too. When it is ready, we share it with you to review.

  • Layout and design built around your content
  • A working contact form so customers can reach you
  • Page titles and descriptions to help search engines

What we need from you: your text, photos and logo, if you have them. We can help if you do not.

04

Refine

You review the website and tell us what to change. We make the updates, test everything, and help you get your site online. Two rounds of revisions are included in every project.

  • Your requested changes, up to two revision rounds
  • Final testing on different screen sizes
  • Guidance on choosing a domain and hosting, and going live

What we need from you: clear feedback, and your final approval to launch.

TIMELINES

How long will it take?

These are typical ranges. Most delays come from waiting for content or feedback, so the faster you reply, the faster we finish.

  • Landing page: usually 3-7 days
  • Full website or portfolio: usually 1-3 weeks
  • E-commerce website: usually 2-4 weeks

Your exact timeline is confirmed in your quote.

GETTING READY

What to prepare before we start.

You do not need everything on this list, but the more you have, the faster things go.

Your content

A short description of your business or idea, your services or products, and your contact details.

Your visuals

Your logo, brand colours and any photos you want to use. If you have none, tell us and we will advise.

Your inspiration

Websites or designs you like, including our concepts. It helps us understand your taste quickly.
