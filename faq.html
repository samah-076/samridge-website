TIME & COST

Timelines and pricing.

How long does a website take?

Landing pages usually take 3-7 days. Full websites take 1-3 weeks, depending on the number of pages and how quickly you send content and feedback.

How much does it cost?

Landing pages start from ₦50,000, full websites from ₦150,000, and e-commerce websites from ₦300,000. The final cost depends on your requirements, and we'll give you a clear quote before we begin.

Are the prices fixed?

The prices shown are starting prices. Your final price depends on the number of pages, the features you need and the amount of content. You will see the full price in your quote before any work starts.

How does payment work?

Payment terms are agreed with you in your quote, before work begins, so there are no surprises. If you have a preference, tell us and we will discuss it.

YOUR PROJECT

Changes, content and going live.

Can I request changes?

Yes. Two rounds of revisions are included in every project. Extra changes beyond that can be arranged for a small fee.

Do you help with hosting?

Yes. We can guide you in choosing a domain and hosting, and help get your website live. Domain and hosting are separate costs paid to the provider you choose.

What do I need to provide?

A short description of your business or idea, your contact details, and your logo and photos if you have them. If you don't have some of these, tell us and we'll advise you on what to do.

Will my website work on phones?

Yes. We design for phones first, because most visitors browse on them, then make sure the site also looks great on tablets and computers.

Will my website show up on Google?

We build every site with a clean structure and proper page titles and descriptions, which helps Google understand it. No one can promise a specific ranking, because that depends on many factors, but we give your site a solid foundation.

ABOUT SAMRIDGE

About our work.

Are the designs on your website real client projects?

No. They are demonstration concepts created to show possible design directions. We state this openly so you know exactly what you are looking at.

Can you copy a design I like?

We can create a design inspired by a concept or style you like and shape it around your brand. We don't copy other people's websites directly.

What types of websites do you build?

Websites for businesses and organisations, landing pages, portfolio websites and e-commerce websites. See the Services page for details.

How do I get started?

Send us a short message through the contact page, or chat with us on WhatsApp. Tell us what you need and we'll reply with a plan, timeline and quote.
